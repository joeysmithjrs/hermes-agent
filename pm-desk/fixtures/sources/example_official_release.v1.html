Gross Domestic Product, 1st Quarter 2026 (Advance Estimate)

July 30, 2026

Real gross domestic product (GDP) increased at an annual rate of 3.1 percent in the first quarter of 2026, according to the advance estimate released today.

The increase primarily reflected increases in consumer spending and nonresidential fixed investment, partly offset by a decrease in inventory investment.
